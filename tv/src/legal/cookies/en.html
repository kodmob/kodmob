What is a cookie?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Browser storage (localStorage) similarly lets a site keep small pieces of information in your browser. This policy covers both.

What we do not use

We do not use advertising, retargeting, social media or analytics cookies. Our pages contain no third-party scripts, pixels or embedded content, so we do not show a cookie consent banner.

Managing cookies

You can view and delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. If you do, the site keeps working; only your language and theme preferences{{#modules.commerce}} and your cart{{/modules.commerce}} are forgotten.
