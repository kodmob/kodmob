When you visit the site

When you open our pages, your browser sends our server your IP address, browser details and the page requested, as with any website. This information may be kept briefly in server logs to run and secure the site. We do not track visits per person or build profiles.

Our security system records the IP address and a request summary only for requests it blocks on suspicion of attack or abuse. These records are deleted after {{retention.security}} days.

Your language and theme preferences are stored in your browser{{#modules.commerce}}; a cookie is used to remember your cart{{/modules.commerce}}.{{#links.cookies}} See our Cookie Policy for details.{{/links.cookies}}

When you write to us

When you submit a contact or enquiry form we receive your name, e-mail address, optional phone number and message. To prevent spam we store an irreversible hash of your IP address (not the address itself) and your browser details with your request.

We use this information only to assess your request and reply to you. Requests are automatically and permanently deleted from our system after {{retention.briefs}} months.{{#links.contactNotice}} Details: Contact and Enquiry Form Notice.{{/links.contactNotice}}

Service providers and transfers

We do not share your personal data with third parties for marketing. It may be transferred only to the following recipients, to the extent required:

  • Hosting and e-mail infrastructure: the provider of the server hosting our site and database and of the infrastructure that sends e-mails.
    • {{#modules.commerce}}
  • Shipping and logistics companies: name, address and phone number to deliver physical goods.
    • {{/modules.commerce}} {{#modules.stripe}}
  • Stripe: payment amount, e-mail address and order number to take card payments. Stripe's servers are located abroad.
    • {{/modules.stripe}} {{#modules.paytr}}
  • PayTR: name, e-mail, phone, address, payment amount and order number to take card payments.
    • {{/modules.paytr}}
  • Authorised authorities: competent public authorities and courts where required by law.

Where a transfer abroad is necessary, we follow the procedures and safeguards of Article 9 of Law No. 6698 and, for visitors from the EU, the GDPR.

External services and links

Our pages contain only links to external services such as social media, messaging (e.g. WhatsApp) and maps. These links send no information until you click them; once you do, that service's own privacy terms apply.

How we protect your data

  • The site is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Only authorised site administrators can access personal data; the admin panel is closed to visitors, can be protected with two-step verification and actions there are logged.
  • IP addresses are not stored in plain form in form records; forms are protected by rate limits and automatic spam protection.
  • Our pages are served with a strict Content Security Policy (CSP); no code is run from external sources.
