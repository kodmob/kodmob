Unsubscribing (right to withdraw)

You can withdraw your consent at any time, without giving reasons and free of charge: use the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of every newsletter or your e-mail app's unsubscribe button. Transfers

Your e-mail address is shared only with the providers of the e-mail infrastructure that sends the newsletter and of the server hosting our site, to the extent needed to provide their service. Where required by law, consents are registered in the Turkish Message Management System (İYS). We never sell or rent our list.

Your subscription data is kept until you unsubscribe. Consent and withdrawal records are kept as proof for the statutory period (three years) after the consent ends and then deleted.
