General

Orders placed on {{site.name}} are prepared and shipped after the order is confirmed. For bank transfers, preparation starts when the payment reaches our account. You can follow your order from the link in the confirmation e-mail; we also e-mail you when it ships.

On receipt

Check the outer packaging when you receive the parcel. We recommend refusing a damaged, crushed or opened parcel, or accepting it only with a damage report signed by the courier. Contact us about goods that arrive damaged; they will be replaced free of charge or refunded.

If you are not at the address, the carrier leaves a notice; parcels not collected in time are returned to us and we will contact you.

Digital goods

Digital goods are delivered electronically by e-mail or on the order page once payment is confirmed; no shipping costs apply.
