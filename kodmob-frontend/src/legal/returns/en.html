Cancelling an order

You can cancel your order before it ships by writing to {{controller.email}} with your order number. Any amount paid is refunded to your payment method within 14 days of the cancellation being confirmed.

Right of withdrawal (14 days)

You may withdraw from the contract within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving reasons or paying a penalty. For orders delivered in several parts, the period starts on the day you receive the last item.

  1. Before the period ends, write to {{controller.email}} with your order number and withdrawal request.
  2. Send the goods, with the invoice and, if possible, the original packaging, to the address we give you within 10 days of your notice.
  3. Within 14 days of receiving your notice, we refund the full amount you paid, including any delivery charge, to the payment method you used.

You are responsible for any loss in value caused by handling the goods beyond what is necessary; examining and trying the goods does not affect your right of withdrawal.

When there is no right of withdrawal

  • goods made to your specifications or personalised,
  • goods that deteriorate quickly or may expire,
  • unsealed goods unsuitable for return for health or hygiene reasons,
  • digital content delivered and services performed instantly online,
  • accommodation, tours, activities and similar services provided on a specific date.

Defective goods

If the goods arrive defective, damaged or different from what you ordered, you may choose under Article 11 of Law No. 6502 to withdraw from the contract, have the price reduced, have them repaired free of charge or replaced. In this case we cover the shipping costs.

Refunds

Approved refunds go to the card used, or for bank transfer and cash on delivery, to the bank account (IBAN) you give us. How long it takes to appear depends on your bank.

Disputes

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may apply to the consumer arbitration committee at your place of residence within the monetary limits set by the Ministry of Trade, or to the consumer court above them.
