Right of withdrawal

Under Article 15 of the Turkish Distance Contracts Regulation there is no right of withdrawal for tours, activities{{#modules.hotels}} and accommodation{{/modules.hotels}} provided on a specific date. The cancellation terms below apply instead.

Cancellations by you

Send your cancellation in writing with your booking number to {{controller.email}}. Unless the booking page states otherwise:

  • cancellations 7 or more days before the service date are refunded in full,
  • cancellations less than 7 days before the service date and no-shows are not refunded.

Date changes are considered subject to availability and price.

Cancellations and changes by us

If we must cancel because of insufficient participants, weather, force majeure or safety, we inform you immediately; if you do not accept another date, you receive a full refund within 14 days. Minor necessary programme changes may occur; for a significant change you may cancel free of charge.

Participants' responsibilities

  • Participants are responsible for ID, passport, visa and health requirements.
  • Please arrive at the meeting point on time; services missed due to lateness are not refunded.
  • Participants must follow the safety instructions of staff during the service.

Refunds

Refunds go to the card used, or for bank transfers to the bank account you give us.
